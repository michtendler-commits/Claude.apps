About

COSMOLYTE, aka Emmett T, is a New York producer and artist who makes electronic music on his own terms. He releases EDM, Nu Disco, and pop that has found its way into the world. COSMOLYTE also created the independent group HollowVale, and he writes and directs short films.

He used to go by the name “ERT.” The name changed; the ambition got bigger.

In 2026, he released his self-titled debut mixtape, COSMOLYTE, a record that doesn’t belong to one sound or one scene. COSMOLYTE produces, DJs, films, scores, and creates, and somehow it all fits together under one name.
