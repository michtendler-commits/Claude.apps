About

COSMOLYTE makes music for the space between stars — glowing synths, cinematic drops, and melodies that feel like a late-night drive through the cosmos.

Blending electronic production with raw emotion, every track is built to be felt as much as heard. Whether it's headphones at 2am or a packed room with the bass rattling the walls, COSMOLYTE is about getting lost and finding something on the way back.
